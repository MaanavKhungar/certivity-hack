- R-0004 p8 [5.3] The international approval mark on the ESA shall be affixed in the place specified on the approval communication form.
- R-0005 p8 [5.3.1] The approval mark shall comprise a circle containing the letter "E" followed by the distinguishing number of the country granting the approval.
- R-0006 p8 [5.3.2] The approval mark shall comprise the number of this Regulation (10), followed by the letter "R", a dash and the approval number, placed to the right of the circle.
- R-0007 p8 [5.5] The approval marking on the ESA, conforming to paragraph 5.3, need not be visible when the ESA is installed in the vehicle; it may be placed, for example, on the rear or inner side of the housing.

- R-0026 p25 [9.1] Rear lamp ESAs approved under this Regulation shall be so manufactured as to conform to the type approved by meeting the requirements set forth in paragraph 6 (paragraph 7 not applicable, as the lamp is not used in REESS charging mode coupled to the power grid).
- R-0027 p26 [9.3.1] For a rear lamp ESA taken from series production, the measured broadband electromagnetic disturbance levels shall not exceed the reference limits prescribed in paragraph 6.5.2.1 by more than 4 dB (60 per cent).
- R-0028 p26 [9.3.2] A rear lamp ESA taken from series production shall not exhibit any degradation relating to the direct control of the vehicle that could be observed by the driver or other road users (e.g. stop lamp or rear position lamp switching off or behaving abnormally) when subjected to a field strength, expressed in V/m, up to 80 per cent of the reference limits prescribed in paragraph 6.8.2.1.
- R-0029 p26 [9.3.3] A rear lamp component/STU taken from series production shall show no degradation of performance of its immunity-related functions (stop lamp, rear position lamp) up to the conducted transient immunity levels given in paragraph 6.9.1 for its 12 V or 24 V supply system.
- R-0030 p26 [9.3.3] A rear lamp component/STU taken from series production shall not exceed the conducted transient emission levels given in paragraph 6.7.1 on its 12 V or 24 V supply lines.
- R-0031 p91 [Annex 7 2.1] During the broadband emission test the rear lamp ESA under test shall be in normal operation mode, preferably at maximum load (e.g. rear position, stop and reversing lamp functions all energised).
- R-0032 p92 [Annex 7 4.1] The broadband emission limits of paragraph 6.5 shall be met throughout the frequency range 30 to 1,000 MHz for measurements performed in an ALSE or OATS.
- R-0033 p96 [Annex 8 2] In case of an active electronic LED driver (or where the exceptions of paragraph 6.10 do not apply), the lamp ESA shall be in normal operation mode, preferably at maximum load, during the narrowband emission test.
- R-0034 p96 [Annex 8 4.1] The narrowband emission limits of paragraph 6.6.2.1 shall be met by the lamp ESA throughout the frequency range 30 to 1,000 MHz, measured in an absorber lined shielded enclosure (ALSE) or open area test site (OATS).
- R-0035 p98 [Annex 9 2.2] During immunity testing, the rear lamp ESA shall be switched on and stimulated to be in normal operation condition, with the stop lamp and rear position lamp functions active.
- R-0040 p108 [Annex 10 1] The ESA (rear lamp LED modules and drive circuitry) shall be immune to conducted transients on the vehicle power supply.
- R-0041 p108 [Annex 10 1] The ESA shall limit the conducted transients it generates on the vehicle power supply.
- R-0044 p108 [Annex 10 3] The emission of transient conducted disturbances generated by the ESA on its 12/24 V supply lines, and on other connections that may be operationally connected to supply lines, shall be measured according to ISO 7637-2 and shall meet the applicable limits.
