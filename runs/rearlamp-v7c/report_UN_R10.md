## Extra, not matched to any expected (21)
- R-0001 p8 [4.2.4] In case of non-replaceable LED modules, the ESA that is (part of) the light source shall be shown, by a Technical Service test report, not to be mechanically interchangeable with any light source according to Regulation No. 37, No. 99 or No. 128; otherwise R10 approval of the ESA shall not be granted.
- R-0002 p8 [4.2.4] In case of a replaceable LED light source, the ESA that is (part of) the light source shall carry an approval number according to Regulation No. 128 (or No. 37 / No. 99); otherwise R10 approval of the ESA shall not be granted.
- R-0004 p8 [5.3.1] The UN R10 approval mark on the ESA shall comprise a circle containing the letter "E", followed by the distinguishing number of the country granting the approval.
- R-0005 p8 [5.3.2] The UN R10 approval mark on the ESA shall comprise, to the right of the circle, the number of this Regulation (10) followed by the letter "R", a dash and the approval number.
- R-0008 p9 [6.1.1.2] The ESA shall be tested for immunity to radiated disturbances and for immunity to conducted disturbances.
- R-0015 p10 [6.7.1. Table 1] Maximum allowed transient pulse amplitude emitted on the supply lines: for 12 V systems, positive +75 V and negative -100 V; for 24 V systems, positive +150 V and negative -450 V.
- R-0024 p25 [9.1] The rear combination lamp (ESA) approved under this Regulation shall be so manufactured as to conform to the type approved by meeting the requirements set forth in paragraph 6.
- R-0025 p26 [9.3.1] In case of an active electronic LED driver (or where the emission tests are not waived under paragraph 6.10), the broadband electromagnetic disturbance levels measured on a production lamp shall not exceed the reference limits prescribed in paragraph 6.5.2.1 by more than 4 dB (60 per cent).
- R-0026 p26 [9.3.1] In case of an active electronic LED driver (or where the emission tests are not waived under paragraph 6.10), the narrowband electromagnetic disturbance levels measured on a production lamp shall not exceed the reference limits prescribed in paragraph 6.6.2.1 by more than 4 dB (60 per cent).
- R-0027 p26 [9.3.2] A production lamp shall not exhibit any degradation relating to the direct control of the vehicle that could be observed by the driver or other road users (e.g. stop lamp or rear position lamp switching off, or the stop lamp activating unexpectedly) when subjected to an electromagnetic field strength (V/m) of up to 80 per cent of the reference limits prescribed in paragraph 6.8.2.1.
- R-0028 p26 [9.3.3] A production lamp shall show no degradation of performance of its immunity-related functions (stop lamp, rear position lamp) when subjected to conducted transient disturbances on the 12 V or 24 V supply lines up to the levels given in paragraph 6.9.1 (ISO 7637-2 test pulses per Annex 10), for the rated supply voltage of the lamp.
- R-0029 p26 [9.3.3] The conducted transient emission of a production lamp on the 12 V or 24 V supply lines shall not exceed the levels given in paragraph 6.7.1 (maximum allowed pulse amplitude per Annex 10), for the rated supply voltage of the lamp.
- R-0032 p91 [Annex 7 1.2] Unless otherwise stated in Annex 7, the rear lamp ESA (LED modules on PCB with drive circuitry) shall be tested for broadband electromagnetic emissions according to CISPR 25, so that compliance with the broadband limits of paragraph 6.5 can be shown. In case of a passive LED drive (resistors/linear only), this applies unless the exception of paragraph 6.10 is claimed.
- R-0033 p92 [Annex 7 4.1] The rear lamp ESA shall comply with the broadband emission limits throughout the frequency range 30 to 1,000 MHz when measured in an ALSE or OATS.
- R-0034 p93 [Annex 7 4.5] For the rear lamp ESA, the maximum of the readings relative to the limit (horizontal and vertical polarisation) in each of the 14 frequency bands shall be taken as the characteristic reading at the frequency at which the measurement was made, and this characteristic reading shall be within the limit.
- R-0035 p96 [Annex 8 1.2] The narrowband electromagnetic emissions of the ESA shall be measured according to CISPR 25, using the ALSE method of paragraph 6.4 of CISPR 25 (or an OATS complying with CISPR 16-1-4), unless otherwise stated in Annex 8.
- R-0036 p96 [Annex 8 4.1] The ESA narrowband emission limits of paragraph 6.6 shall be met throughout the frequency range 30 to 1,000 MHz for measurements performed in an ALSE or OATS.
- R-0037 p97 [Annex 8 4.5] For each of the 14 frequency bands, the characteristic reading of the ESA shall be the maximum of the readings relative to the limit for horizontal and vertical antenna polarisation, and this reading shall not exceed the limit.
- R-0038 p97 [Annex 9 1.2.1] The ESA (rear lamp with LED modules and drive electronics) shall comply with the radiated immunity requirements using any combination of the following test methods, at the manufacturer's discretion, provided that the full frequency range of 20 to 6,000 MHz is covered: absorber chamber test (ISO 11452-2), TEM cell testing (ISO 11452-3), bulk current injection testing (ISO 11452-4), stripline testing (ISO 11452-5) or reverberation chamber test (ISO 11452-11).
- R-0044 p108 [Annex 10 2] The rear lamp ESA shall be immune to ISO 7637-2 test pulses 1, 2a, 2b, 3a and 3b applied to any other connections of the ESA that may be operationally connected to supply lines (e.g. switched function inputs such as stop or position lamp feeds).
- R-0046 p108 [Annex 10 3] The transient conducted disturbances that the rear lamp ESA generates on its 12/24 V supply lines, and on other connections that may be operationally connected to supply lines, shall be measured according to ISO 7637-2 and limited.
